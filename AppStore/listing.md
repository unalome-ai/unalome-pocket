## Description (4000)

```
Your AI. In your pocket.

Unalome Pocket runs open AI models directly on your device. What you type, say or show it never leaves your iPhone, iPad or Mac. There is no account, no subscription and no server.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Chats are created and stored on your device only.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Works offline once a model is installed. Try it in airplane mode.

PICK A MODEL THAT FITS YOUR DEVICE
• A curated catalog of open models, including Qwen 3.5, Gemma and LFM.
• The app checks your device's memory and recommends the best fit.
• Each model shows what it can do: text, images, voice, thinking.
• Add other MLX models by their Hugging Face ID.
• On devices with Apple Intelligence, start right away with Apple's built-in model, no download needed.

CHAT
• Fast streaming replies, with speed shown as it writes.
• Ask about a photo or a picture from your camera with vision models.
• Dictate with on-device speech recognition.
• Optional thinking mode for harder questions.
• Personas: your own instructions and tone for different tasks.
• Markdown, tables and code blocks, rendered cleanly.
• Search, rename and delete saved conversations.

EVERYWHERE YOU ARE
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a Control Center button to ask by voice.
• Siri and Shortcuts: start a chat or ask a question.
• Download progress in the Dynamic Island.
• Built for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with keyboard shortcuts.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Unalome Pocket is free, with no in-app purchases. The source code is public under the Apache 2.0 licence.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Models are large downloads, from about 0.7 GB to several GB. Wi-Fi is recommended.
• Larger models need more memory. The app tells you what your device can run.
• Answers are generated by AI and can be wrong. Check anything important.
• Each model comes with its own licence, shown before you download it.
```
