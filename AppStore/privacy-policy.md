# Unalome Pocket privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Unalome Pocket runs AI models on your own device. We built it so that we never receive
your data. This page explains exactly what that means.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting
service. We operate no server that the app sends your data to.

## What stays on your device

- Your conversations, including text, images you attach and personas you create.
- The models you download.
- Your settings.

These are stored in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting a conversation or
the app removes them. If you use iCloud or computer backups of your device, your
conversations may be included in those backups under Apple's terms; downloaded models
are excluded.

## Voice, camera and photos

- **Voice input** uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is turned into text
  on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Camera and photos** are used only when you attach an image to a message. The image
  is read by the model on your device.

You can withdraw these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

## When the app uses the network

The app connects to the internet in one situation: when you download a model or look
one up by its ID. These requests go to **Hugging Face** (huggingface.co), which hosts
the model files. As with any download, Hugging Face sees your IP address and the file
you request. It does not receive your conversations. See
[Hugging Face's privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).

Chatting does not use the network. The app works in airplane mode once a model is
installed.

## Apple's built-in model

On devices that support it, you can chat with Apple's on-device model. It runs on your
device through Apple's system frameworks, under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Reporting a response

"Report this response" opens an email draft in your mail app. Nothing is sent unless you
send that email, and you can edit it first. If you send it, we receive what the email
contains and your email address, and use them only to look into the report.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
